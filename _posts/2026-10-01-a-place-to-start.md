There is something nice about having a space of your own on the internet. It doesn't need to do everything. It just needs to feel like somewhere you want to return to.

This is the beginning of that space: a place for notes, a place for projects, and enough room for both to change over time.

## Keeping it simple

The idea is straightforward. Write when there is something worth writing down. Share a project when there is something worth showing. Let the rest take shape along the way.

> A website doesn't have to be finished to be useful.

For now, a small beginning is enough.

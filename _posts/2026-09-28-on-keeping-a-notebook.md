Not every idea arrives fully formed. Sometimes it is a sentence, a question, or a link that you want to come back to.

A notebook gives those things somewhere to land. It takes the pressure off remembering everything, and leaves a record of what caught your attention.

## A few useful habits

- Write the question before trying to find the answer.
- Keep enough context that a note will still make sense later.
- Return to old notes occasionally. The connections are often the interesting part.

The same is true of a little blog. A note can be short. It can be unfinished. It can simply be something you learned today.

The point is to leave a trail.

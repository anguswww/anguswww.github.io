A personal website built with Jekyll. Three simple spaces: an introduction, a collection of notes, and a home for projects.

## The idea

Keep the interface quiet and give the content room to breathe. A readable column, ordinary links, and just ten lines of CSS.

## Built with

- **Jekyll** for turning Markdown into pages.
- **HTML and CSS** for a responsive, lightweight interface.
- **GitHub Pages** for hosting when the site is published.

The notes and projects are ordinary Markdown files, so the site can grow one small addition at a time.
